Frequently Asked Questions

Can my dog still go potty while using the cart?

Yes! The flexible ergonomic saddle gently supports their pelvis while leaving open clearance so your dog can comfortably go potty on walks without needing to be unstrapped.

How long does custom fabrication take?

Because our CAD files and cut sheets are generated automatically from your measurements, your cart is custom printed, cut, and typically ships within 3 to 5 business days from measurement confirmation.

What if the fit is slightly off?

The frame includes ±1.5 inches of sliding adjustments on the side rails and axle struts. If a component needs resizing, your order includes one complimentary set of replacement 3D-printed adjustment joints within 30 days of delivery (customer covers return shipping of original parts, or shipping fees apply). Complete structural frame rebuilds due to initial customer measurement discrepancy are subject to a remanufacturing materials fee.
